Self-hosted Noto Sans SC, Noto Serif SC and Geist WOFF2 subsets.
These are the same font binaries used by the previously validated Next.js build,
with their original unicode ranges and fallback metrics in app/styles/fonts.css.
Upstream font families: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl
Licenses: OFL-NotoSansSC.txt, OFL-NotoSerifSC.txt, OFL-Geist.txt.
